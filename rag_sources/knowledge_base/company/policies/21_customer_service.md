# Customer Service

- Category: Company policy
- Owner: Amal Al-Dakheel, Head of Customer Service (EMP-0034)
- Effective date: 2025-03-01
- Version: 2.1
- Last reviewed: 2026-09-30
- Applies to: Customer Service and store staff
- Classification: Internal

## 1. Service Promise

Customers receive a clear answer fast and a fair solution. Customer Service is led by the Head of Customer Service, Amal Al-Dakheel.

## 2. Channels and Hours

| Channel | Hours |
|---|---|
| Phone | Daily 08:00 to 24:00 |
| Chat in the Mobile App and on the website | Daily 08:00 to 24:00 |
| Email (customerservice@nakhla-retail.example) | Answered within one business day |
| In store | During store opening hours |

Business customers are served by their account manager (see KB-POL-12).

## 3. Response Targets

| Request | Target |
|---|---|
| First response to chat or phone | Within 2 minutes |
| First response to email | Within 4 working hours |
| Order status question | Answered at first contact |
| Complaint resolution | Within 3 business days |
| Refund after an accepted return | Within 7 business days (see KB-POL-09) |

## 4. Identity Verification

Before discussing an order, a loyalty balance or personal data, the agent verifies the customer using the order number and the registered phone number or email address. Agents never read contact details out to a caller.

## 5. Complaints

A complaint is recorded, acknowledged and resolved by the first agent where possible. An unresolved complaint is escalated to the Head of Customer Service after 3 business days and to the Chief Commercial Officer after 10 business days.

## 6. Quality and Measures

- Customer satisfaction target: 4.5 out of 5 on the post-contact survey.
- The team reviews return reasons, late deliveries and repeat complaints every month and reports to the Chief Commercial Officer.

## 7. Service Recovery

When the company is at fault, the agent may offer a refund of the delivery fee or a store credit of up to SAR 100 without approval. Larger gestures are approved by the Head of Customer Service.
