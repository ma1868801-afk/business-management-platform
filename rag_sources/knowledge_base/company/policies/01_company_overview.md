## 1. Who We Are

Nakhla Retail Group is a Saudi multi-category retailer headquartered in Riyadh. The company was founded in 2015, opened its Distribution Center in Riyadh on 2015-08-01 and its first store, Riyadh Olaya Flagship, on 2016-03-15. The name Nakhla means date palm, a symbol of generosity and resilience in Saudi culture. All names and figures in this document set are fictional.

## 2. The Network in Brief

- Nine locations: eight retail stores (two Flagship, four Standard, two Express) and one Distribution Center in Riyadh (`RYD-DC`).
- Regions: Central (Riyadh Olaya, Riyadh Malqa and the Distribution Center), Western (Jeddah Tahlia, Jeddah Obhur and Madinah Quba), Eastern (Dammam Faisaliyah and Al Khobar Corniche) and Southern (Abha City).
- Four sales channels: In-Store, Online (website), Mobile App and B2B (contract business customers). Online, Mobile App and B2B orders are fulfilled from the Distribution Center, so their sales are attributed to `RYD-DC`.
- Details of each location are in the branch profiles (see the branch network policy, KB-POL-04).

## 3. The Range

- Eight product lines with 15 SKUs each, 120 SKUs in total: Consumer Electronics (ELEC), Home Appliances (APPL), Fashion and Apparel (FASH), Grocery and Gourmet (GROC), Beauty and Fragrance (BEAU), Sports and Outdoor (SPRT), Kids and School (KIDS) and Home Decor and Furnishing (HOME).
- Each product line is supplied by two contracted suppliers, 16 suppliers in total (SUP-01 to SUP-16).
- Each store carries between 80 and 100 SKUs. The Distribution Center holds all 120.

## 4. Customers

- Individual customers, some of them members of the Nakhla Rewards loyalty programme (tiers None, Silver, Gold and Platinum).
- Twelve contract business customers (CUST-B001 to CUST-B012) served by three B2B account managers.

## 5. Business Facts

- Consumer Electronics is the line with the highest net revenue; Grocery and Gourmet sells the most units; Beauty and Fragrance has the highest gross margin percentage; Fashion and Apparel has the highest return rate.
- Riyadh Olaya Flagship is the highest-revenue store and Abha City Express the smallest.
- Online and Mobile App orders together were about 14 percent of net revenue in 2024 and about 19 percent in 2025, and rose to about 24 percent in January to October 2026.
- Exact figures always come from the sales database; the numbers in this document are indicative.

## 6. Conventions

- Currency: Saudi riyal (SAR). VAT is 15 percent; revenue and prices in internal reporting exclude VAT.
- Time zone: Asia/Riyadh (UTC+3). The retail week runs from Sunday to Saturday and the weekend is Friday and Saturday.
- Reporting reference date used in this knowledge base: 2026-10-31.
