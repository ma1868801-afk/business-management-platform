# Code of Conduct

- Document ID: KB-POL-19
- Category: Company policy
- Owner: Abdulaziz Al-Rashid, Chief Executive Officer (EMP-0001)
- Effective date: 2024-01-01
- Version: 1.4
- Last reviewed: 2026-09-30
- Applies to: All employees, temporary staff and contractors
- Classification: Internal

## 1. Our Standards

Every employee acts with integrity, respects colleagues, customers and suppliers, and protects the company's information and property. The code applies to all employees, temporary staff and contractors.

## 2. Honest Dealing

- Prices, promotions and product claims are stated truthfully.
- Employees do not accept or give bribes or improper payments.
- Records of sales, stock and expenses are accurate and complete.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

An employee declares to the HR Director any interest in a supplier, customer or competitor and does not take part in decisions about it. Employees may not work for a supplier or competitor.

## 4. Gifts and Hospitality

Gifts and hospitality from suppliers or customers are accepted only if their value is below SAR 500 and they are not offered during a tender or contract negotiation. Gifts above this value are returned or handed to the HR Director.

## 5. Respect at Work

Discrimination, harassment and bullying are not tolerated. Managers treat reports fairly and decisions on hiring, pay and promotion are based on merit.

## 6. Confidentiality and Use of Data

- Employees use customer and employee data only for their job (see KB-POL-16).
- Company information, contracts and prices are not shared outside the company without approval.
- Company systems, including the AI assistant, are used for business purposes.

## 7. Speaking Up

Concerns are raised with the line manager, the HR Director or the confidential reporting line run by the Information Security Officer. Reports made in good faith are protected from retaliation.

## 8. Consequences

Breaches lead to disciplinary action in line with the Saudi Labor Law, from a written warning up to dismissal. Serious breaches may be reported to the authorities.
