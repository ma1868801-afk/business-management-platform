## 1. Accepted Payment Methods

| Method | Where available |
|---|---|
| Mada | All channels |
| Credit Card | All channels |
| Apple Pay | All channels |
| STC Pay | All channels |
| Cash | In stores only |
| Cash on Delivery | Online delivery only |
| Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) | Online and Mobile App, through approved providers |
| Bank Transfer | B2B customers on credit terms |

## 2. VAT

- The standard VAT rate is 15 percent and applies to all sales.
- Prices displayed to customers include VAT. Internal price lists, revenue and margin reports exclude VAT.
- The order total paid by the customer equals the subtotal plus 15 percent VAT plus any delivery fee.
- Net revenue in all management reports is the value of Completed orders after line discounts, excluding VAT and excluding delivery fees.

## 3. Invoices

- Every sale generates a tax invoice that shows the VAT amount.
- Online and Mobile App customers receive the invoice by email; store customers receive a printed or digital receipt.
- B2B customers receive a monthly consolidated statement and individual tax invoices.

## 4. Refunds and Reversals

Refunds follow the returns policy (KB-POL-09). VAT on refunded items is reversed in the same period.

## 5. Currency

All sales are in Saudi riyal (SAR). Supplier invoices may be in SAR, US dollars, euros or UAE dirhams; the purchase order value is converted to SAR at the order date for reporting.

## 6. Cash Handling

Stores bank cash takings daily. The Branch Manager reconciles the till at close and reports differences to the Financial Controller.
