- Applies to: Business customers and account managers
- Classification: Internal

## 1. Scope

B2B sales serve organisations that have signed a customer agreement with Nakhla Retail Group: hotels, hospitals, schools, contractors, caterers and staff associations. There are 12 contract business customers (CUST-B001 to CUST-B012). They buy through the B2B channel, their orders are fulfilled from the Distribution Center and recorded under `RYD-DC`.

## 2. Contract Terms

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Initial term | 12 or 24 months, as stated in the agreement |
| Renewal | Automatic for successive terms of 12 months unless either party gives written notice 60 days before the term ends |
| Contract discount | Between 5 and 12 percent, set per customer; applied as the line discount on every B2B order |
| Credit limit | Between SAR 150,000 and SAR 1,200,000, set per customer |
| Payment terms | 30, 45 or 60 days from the invoice date |
| Payment method | Bank Transfer |
| Delivery | Free of charge to the customer's site; no delivery fee applies |
| Loyalty | Business customers are not Nakhla Rewards members; their tier is None |

## 3. Credit Control

- The customer's open invoices must stay within the credit limit. An order that would exceed the limit is held until a payment is received or the Finance Manager approves a temporary increase.
- Invoices not paid by the due date are reminded after 5 days. After 30 days overdue, new orders are held until the balance is settled.
- Credit limit increases are proposed by the account manager and approved by the Finance Manager up to SAR 100,000 and by the Chief Financial Officer above that.

## 4. Pricing Rules

- The contract discount replaces promotional and member discounts. It never exceeds the discount cap of the product line.
- Prices are the current list prices; the discount is applied at order entry.
- Quotations are valid for 14 days.

## 5. Account Management

Three account managers look after the accounts and report to the Chief Commercial Officer: Bandar Al-Rasheed (Central Region), Lina Al-Najjar (Western and Southern Regions) and Turki Al-Harthi (Eastern Region). Each account has a review meeting every quarter covering spend, payment behaviour and renewal.
