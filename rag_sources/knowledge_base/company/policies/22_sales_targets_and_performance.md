# Sales Targets and Performance

- Last reviewed: 2026-09-30
- Applies to: Finance, stores and Distribution Center
- Classification: Internal

## 1. Purpose

Sales targets give each location a clear monthly goal and let management see early where performance falls behind.

## 2. How Targets Are Set

- There is one target for each of the nine locations for each month.
- From January 2025, the target of a month equals the net revenue target of the same month of the previous year multiplied by 1.1 (10 percent growth).
- Targets for 2024 were set in the 2024 budget.
- The Distribution Center target covers Online, Mobile App and B2B sales.
- The Chief Financial Officer and the Chief Executive Officer approve the targets each year; the Finance Manager maintains them.

## 3. Measuring Attainment

Attainment = net revenue (Completed orders, excluding VAT) of the location in the month divided by the target of the month. Net revenue follows the standard definition in the data governance policy (KB-POL-17).

| Attainment | Rating |
|---|---|
| Below 90 percent | Below target |
| 90 to 110 percent | On target |
| Above 110 percent | Above target |

## 4. Review Process

- The Finance Manager publishes attainment for all locations on the third working day after month end.
- The Chief Operating Officer reviews results with the Branch Managers each month.
- A location that is below target for two months in a row prepares a recovery plan with the Chief Operating Officer.
- A location that is above target for three months in a row is reviewed for lessons that can be shared.

## 5. Incentives

Branch Managers and store teams are recognised for attainment above 100 percent over the quarter. Bonus rules are part of employment contracts and are not published in this document.

## 6. Adjustments

Targets are not changed during the year, except for the closure of a location or a major event approved by the Chief Executive Officer.
