## 1. The Distribution Center

The Riyadh Distribution Center (`RYD-DC`) is an 18,000 sqm warehouse that opened on 2015-08-01. It employs 95 people and operates around the clock. It receives supplier deliveries, supplies the eight stores and fulfils Online, Mobile App and B2B orders. It holds all 120 SKUs.

## 3. Inbound Process

1. The supplier delivers within the appointment slot. Deliveries are matched to an open purchase order.
2. The goods are counted and inspected for damage, quantity and expiry dates.
3. Accepted goods are put away and the stock record is updated the same day.
4. Differences between the delivery and the purchase order are recorded as a partial receipt and reported to the Procurement Manager.
5. Damaged goods are quarantined and the Supplier Quality Specialist is informed.

## 4. Outbound Process

- Orders are picked, packed and dispatched on the day they are confirmed when confirmed before 15:00; later orders go the next morning.
- Store replenishment is planned by the Inventory Planning team and shipped on fixed routes.
- Online, Mobile App and B2B orders are handed to the delivery fleet and tracked until delivery.

## 5. Storage Rules

- Products with a shelf life are stored and issued first-expired-first-out.
- Food items are stored in a clean, dry area; items that need cooling are kept in the cold room.
- Electronics and high-value items are stored in a secured area with access limited to authorised staff.
- Stock locations are scanned at every move.

## 6. Counts and Accuracy

- The Distribution Center takes a stock snapshot every Saturday.
- A-class SKUs are cycle counted every month and all stock is counted once a year.
- The inventory accuracy target is 98 percent of locations counted without difference.

## 7. Returns and Damage

- Customer returns arrive at the Distribution Center or the stores and are inspected. Sellable items return to stock. Defective items and items damaged in transit are not restocked.
- Damage in the warehouse is recorded as a stock adjustment with reason Damage.

## 8. Peak Periods

Before Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr and White Friday the Distribution Center adds shifts and temporary staff. The Distribution Center Manager agrees the plan with the Chief Operating Officer 8 weeks before the event.
