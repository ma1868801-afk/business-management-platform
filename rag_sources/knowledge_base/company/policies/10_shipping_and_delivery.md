# Shipping and Delivery

- Document ID: KB-POL-10
- Category: Company policy
- Owner: Sara Al-Mansour, Head of E-commerce (EMP-0006)
- Effective date: 2025-03-01
- Version: 1.5
- Last reviewed: 2026-09-30
- Applies to: Online and Mobile App customers, Distribution Center
- Classification: Public

## 1. Delivery Coverage

Online and Mobile App orders are delivered from the Riyadh Distribution Center to addresses in Saudi Arabia. Orders of contract business customers (B2B) are delivered to the customer's site.

## 2. Delivery Fee

| Situation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Online or Mobile App order with a subtotal below SAR 250, customer not Gold or Platinum | SAR 25 |
| Online or Mobile App order with a subtotal of SAR 250 or more | Free |
| Online or Mobile App order by a Gold or Platinum member | Free |
| In-Store purchase | No delivery fee |
| B2B order | Free |

The subtotal is the order value after discounts and excluding VAT and the delivery fee.

## 3. Delivery Times

| Destination | Standard delivery |
|---|---|
| Riyadh | 1 to 2 business days |
| Jeddah, Dammam, Al Khobar, Madinah | 2 to 3 business days |
| Other cities | 3 to 5 business days |

Delivery times start when the order is confirmed. In peak events such as Ramadan and White Friday, add one business day.

## 4. Order Tracking and Failed Delivery

- Customers receive tracking updates by SMS and in the app.
- After two failed delivery attempts the order is returned to the Distribution Center and the customer is contacted. The order may be cancelled and refunded.

## 5. Cancellation

Customers can cancel an order free of charge before dispatch. After dispatch the order must be returned under the returns policy (KB-POL-09).

## 6. Responsibility

The Fleet and Delivery Supervisor at the Distribution Center manages deliveries. The Head of E-commerce owns the customer-facing delivery promises.
