## 1. Responsibility

The Chief Operating Officer is responsible for health and safety. Each Branch Manager and the Distribution Center Manager appoint a safety officer for their location.

## 3. Fire Safety

- Exits are never blocked and are checked every day.
- Fire extinguishers and alarms are tested every month; evacuation drills are held twice a year.
- Customers and staff gather at the assembly point of the location.

## 4. Store Safety

- Floors are kept clean and dry. Spills are cleaned at once.
- Shelves are not overloaded and heavy items are stored low.
- Staff use ladders and trolleys instead of climbing on shelves.

## 5. Warehouse Safety

- Staff wear safety shoes and high-visibility vests in storage areas; helmets are required where racks are loaded.
- Forklift drivers hold a valid licence issued by the company.
- Manual lifting is limited to 25 kg; heavier items are lifted with equipment or by two people.

## 6. Heat Safety

In summer, outdoor work such as deliveries and loading is limited between 12:00 and 15:00, water is available at every location and staff report signs of heat stress at once.

## 7. Food Safety

Grocery products are checked every day for expiry dates and storage temperature. Chilled products stay between 0 and 5 degrees Celsius. Expired products are removed from sale and recorded as an Expiry adjustment.

## 8. Incidents

Every injury, near miss or fire is reported to the Branch Manager the same day and recorded within 24 hours. The HR Business Partner follows up and the Chief Operating Officer reviews the incidents each month.
