## 1. Structure

Nakhla Retail Group is led by the Chief Executive Officer, who reports to the Board of Directors. The Chief Financial Officer, the Chief Operating Officer, the Chief Commercial Officer and the Chief Technology and Data Officer report to the CEO. Branch managers, the Distribution Center Manager, the HR Director and the Head of Supply Chain report to the Chief Operating Officer.

## 5. Decision Rights

| Decision | Decided by |
|---|---|
| Annual budget and sales targets | Chief Financial Officer with the Chief Executive Officer |
| Product line promotions | Chief Commercial Officer on the proposal of the Head of Marketing |
| Purchase orders | By value: see the procurement policy (KB-POL-14) |
| New supplier or contract renewal | Head of Supply Chain with the Chief Operating Officer |
| Store staffing and rosters | Branch Manager within the headcount agreed with the Chief Operating Officer |
| Data access for confidential records | Information Security Officer and HR Director |

Employee records with titles, hire dates and reporting lines are in the employee rosters.
