# Pricing and Discounts

- Document ID: KB-POL-06
- Category: Company policy
- Owner: Noura Al-Fahad, Chief Commercial Officer (EMP-0004)
- Effective date: 2025-01-01
- Version: 2.1
- Last reviewed: 2026-09-30
- Applies to: Commercial, finance and stores
- Classification: Internal

## 1. Price Setting

- List prices are set per SKU by the Category Managers and approved by the Chief Commercial Officer.
- List prices in internal systems exclude VAT. Prices shown to customers include 15 percent VAT.
- A SKU's list price can change at most twice in a calendar year, and each change is limited to 6 percent.
- Selling price is never below unit cost.

## 2. Discount Caps by Product Line

No discount on an order line, whether from a promotion or a member benefit, may exceed the cap of its product line:

Consumer Electronics 25 percent, Home Appliances 25 percent, Fashion and Apparel 50 percent, Grocery and Gourmet 20 percent, Beauty and Fragrance 30 percent, Sports and Outdoor 40 percent, Kids and School 35 percent, Home Decor and Furnishing 40 percent.

Store-wide promotions never exceed 20 percent, which is the lowest line cap (Grocery and Gourmet).

## 3. Types of Discount

| Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Promotional discount | Set by a promotion on the promotions calendar (KB-POL-07). Applies during the promotion dates and, where stated, to a product line, a channel or a minimum basket. |
| Member discount | Gold members receive 5 percent and Platinum members 10 percent on lines that carry no promotional discount (KB-POL-08). |
| Contract discount | B2B customers receive the discount stated in their agreement, between 5 and 12 percent (KB-POL-12). |
| Clearance | Markdown of discontinued, declining or dead stock, approved by the Chief Commercial Officer, within the line cap. |

## 4. Combining Discounts

- A promotional discount replaces the member discount; the two never add up.
- A contract discount applies to B2B orders instead of promotions and member discounts.
- Only one discount applies to a line.

## 5. Approval

Promotions are proposed by the Head of Marketing, reviewed for margin by the Finance Manager and approved by the Chief Commercial Officer. A discount above the line cap is not allowed under any approval.

## 6. Price Matching

The company does not match competitor prices. A pricing error is corrected at the till and the customer receives the lower of the displayed and the correct price for one unit.
