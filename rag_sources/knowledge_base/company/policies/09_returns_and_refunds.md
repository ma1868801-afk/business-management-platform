# Returns and Refunds

- Document ID: KB-POL-09
- Category: Company policy
- Owner: Amal Al-Dakheel, Head of Customer Service (EMP-0034)
- Effective date: 2025-03-01
- Version: 2.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-09-30
- Applies to: Customers and customer-facing staff
- Classification: Public

## 1. Return Window

- Customers may return an item within 14 days of the order date.
- Items returned because they are defective may be returned within 30 days of the order date.
- Returns can be made at any store or by arranging a courier collection through Customer Service.

## 2. Conditions

- The item is unused and in its original packaging, with the order number or receipt.
- Opened perishable groceries and opened personal-care or fragrance items cannot be returned unless defective.
- Custom or special orders cannot be returned unless defective.

## 3. Return Reasons

Returns are recorded with one of six reasons: Defective, Wrong Item, Changed Mind, Late Delivery, Not as Described and Damaged in Transit.

## 4. Refunds

- The refund goes to the original payment method within 7 business days of the item being accepted.
- Orders paid by Cash on Delivery are refunded by bank transfer or as Nakhla store credit, at the customer's choice.
- The refund includes the VAT paid on the item. Internal reports record refunds excluding VAT.
- The delivery fee is refunded only when the reason is Defective, Wrong Item or Damaged in Transit.
- Loyalty points earned on a returned purchase are reversed.

## 5. Restocking

Returned items go back to sellable stock after inspection, except items returned as Defective and items returned as Damaged in Transit, which are not restocked.

## 6. Full and Partial Returns

If every item of an order is returned, the order status becomes Returned. If only some items are returned, the order stays Completed and each return is recorded against the order line.

## 7. Return Rate Monitoring

The Head of Customer Service reviews return rates by product line every month. Fashion and Apparel has the highest return rate and Grocery and Gourmet the lowest. Defective items are about one fifth of all returns.
