## 1. Trading Seasons

The commercial calendar has ten seasons: Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Hajj and Eid al-Adha, White Friday, National Day, Founding Day, Back to School, Summer, Winter and Regular. Each day belongs to one season only. When windows overlap, the season higher in this list wins: White Friday, Eid al-Fitr, Hajj and Eid al-Adha, Ramadan, National Day, Founding Day, Back to School, Summer, Winter. Days outside any event are Regular.

Islamic dates move about 11 days earlier each year. Dates up to October 2026 are confirmed; later dates, such as White Friday 2026 (20 to 29 November), are planning estimates.

## 2. Season Definitions

| Season | Definition |
|---|---|
| Ramadan | 1 to 29/30 Ramadan; the last 7 days are classified as Eid al-Fitr by precedence |
| Eid al-Fitr | last 7 days of Ramadan plus 1 to 3 Shawwal |
| Hajj and Eid al-Adha | Arafat Day minus 4 days to Arafat Day plus 5 days |
| White Friday | Friday one week before White Friday to the Sunday after |
| National Day | 18 to 24 September; public holiday on 23 September |
| Founding Day | 20 to 23 February; public holiday on 22 February |
| Back to School | 1 August to 15 September |
| Summer | 1 June to 31 August, lowest precedence among events (August is overridden by Back to School) |

## 4. Public Holidays

Founding Day (22 February), the Eid al-Fitr holiday, Arafat Day, the Eid al-Adha holiday and National Day (23 September) are public holidays.

## 5. Promotion Programme

The company runs 40 promotions between 2024 and 2026, aligned to the seasons: Winter camping clearance, Home Refresh before Ramadan, Founding Day Rewards, Ramadan Kareem and Ramadan Grocery Basket Deals, Eid fragrance and fashion offers, Summer Cooling Sale, Eid al-Adha Celebration, Back to School Savings, National Day offers and White Friday (store-wide and Electronics). Promotion discounts respect the caps of the pricing policy (KB-POL-06). About 22 percent of orders carry a promotion, concentrated in the event windows.

## 6. Preparation Timeline

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| 12 weeks before an event | Demand Planner prepares the forecast and the Category Managers confirm the range. |
| Supplier lead time plus 2 weeks before | Procurement places the purchase orders for the event. |
| 4 weeks before | Marketing publishes the promotion plan to stores. |
| 2 weeks before | Opening hours and staffing are announced. |
| During the event | Daily review of sales against plan and of stock-outs. |
