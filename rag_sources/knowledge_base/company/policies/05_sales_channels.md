## 1. Channels

| Channel | Description | Fulfilment | Typical payment methods |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-Store | Purchases at the eight stores | Customer takes the goods | Mada, Credit Card, Apple Pay, STC Pay, Cash |
| Online | Purchases on the Nakhla website | Delivered from the Distribution Center | Mada, Credit Card, Apple Pay, STC Pay, Cash on Delivery, Buy Now Pay Later |
| Mobile App | Purchases in the Nakhla Rewards app | Delivered from the Distribution Center | Mada, Credit Card, Apple Pay, STC Pay, Buy Now Pay Later |
| B2B | Orders from contract business customers | Delivered from the Distribution Center | Bank Transfer on credit terms |

## 2. Attribution

- In-Store orders are attributed to the store.
- Online, Mobile App and B2B orders are attributed to the Distribution Center (`RYD-DC`).
- About 8 percent of in-store transactions are anonymous walk-in sales without a customer account.

## 3. Order Lifecycle

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Processing | Order accepted and being prepared; only orders of the last three days of the reporting period are in this status. |
| Completed | Order delivered or collected and paid. Only Completed orders count as revenue. |
| Cancelled | Order cancelled before completion. Cancellations are more frequent online and in the app than in stores. |
| Returned | Order returned in full. |

Partial returns leave the order Completed and are recorded as returns.

## 4. Order Numbers

Orders have a business number in the form SO-YYYY-NNNNNNN, for example SO-2025-0004512. Purchase orders to suppliers use the form PO-YYYY-NNNN.

## 5. Channel Rules

- Delivery fees apply only to Online and Mobile App orders (see the shipping policy, KB-POL-10).
- Loyalty points are earned on all channels except B2B.
- B2B prices follow the customer's contract discount (see KB-POL-12).
- The digital share of net revenue (Online plus Mobile App) is a key management measure: about 14 percent in 2024, 19 percent in 2025 and 24 percent in January to October 2026.
