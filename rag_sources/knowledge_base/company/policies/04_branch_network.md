## 2. Store Formats

| Format | Locations | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flagship Store | RYD-OLY, JED-THL | Largest stores with the full range of the eight product lines; lead the Ramadan and White Friday campaigns. |
| Standard Store | RYD-MLQ, DMM-FSL, KHB-COR, MED-QBA | Neighbourhood stores with a broad range tuned to local demand. |
| Express Store | JED-OBH, ABH-CTY | Compact stores with a focused range; they open at 11:00. |
| Distribution Center | RYD-DC | Central warehouse that supplies the stores and fulfils Online, Mobile App and B2B orders. |

## 3. Range and Stock

- Each store holds between 80 and 100 of the 120 SKUs; the range is wider in the larger stores. The Distribution Center holds all 120 SKUs.
- The Distribution Center receives most supplier deliveries. Store replenishment follows the inventory policy (KB-POL-13).

## 4. Revenue Attribution

Sales in the eight stores belong to the store where the sale was made. Online, Mobile App and B2B sales belong to the Distribution Center. Rankings of stores therefore exclude the Distribution Center.

## 5. Opening Hours

Flagship and Standard stores open daily from 10:00 to 23:00. Express stores open daily from 11:00 to 23:00. The Distribution Center operates around the clock. Special hours during Ramadan, Eid and White Friday are set by the Chief Operating Officer and announced two weeks in advance.
