## 1. Mission

To make everyday and special-occasion shopping in Saudi Arabia easier, fairer and more rewarding for every family and business, in stores, online and in the app.

## 2. Vision

To be the most trusted multi-category retailer in the Kingdom, known for dependable availability, honest prices and service that respects the customer's time.

## 3. Values

| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Customer first | We design every process around the customer's time and trust. A fair return is accepted without argument. |
| Integrity | We state prices honestly, deal fairly with suppliers and report numbers as they are. |
| Heritage and community | We celebrate Saudi seasons and traditions such as Ramadan, Eid, Founding Day and National Day, and we support local suppliers. |
| Excellence | We measure our work, learn from the data and improve every week. |
| Teamwork | Stores, the Distribution Center and head office succeed together. |
| Responsibility | We protect people, customer data and the environment. |

## 4. Strategic Priorities for 2026

1. Grow the digital share of net revenue (Online plus Mobile App) toward 24 percent.
2. Raise on-time delivery from suppliers to the 92 percent target.
3. Reduce dead stock and overstock while keeping stock-outs low.
4. Grow the Nakhla Rewards programme and repeat purchases.
5. Meet or exceed the monthly sales target at every location.
