# Human Resources Policy

- Category: Company policy
- Owner: Hind Al-Shammari, HR Director (EMP-0018)
- Effective date: 2026-01-01
- Version: 3.2
- Last reviewed: 2026-09-30
- Applies to: All employees
- Classification: Internal

## 1. Working Time

- The standard working week is 48 hours, up to 8 hours a day, six days a week for store and warehouse staff on rotating rosters, and five days (Sunday to Thursday) for head office.
- During Ramadan, working time for Muslim employees is reduced to 6 hours a day or 36 hours a week.
- Overtime is paid at 150 percent of the basic hourly wage and needs the manager's approval in advance.

## 2. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 90 days. Either party may end the employment during probation with written notice. The line manager and the HR Business Partner review performance before probation ends.

## 3. Annual Leave

- Annual leave accrues from the first day of employment.
- During years 1 to 5 of service, an employee earns 1.75 days for each completed month (21 days a year).
- After 5 years of service, an employee earns 2.5 days for each completed month (30 days a year).
- Example: an employee who started on 2026-03-01 has completed 8 months by 2026-10-31 and has earned 14 days of annual leave (8 x 1.75).
- Leave is requested through the manager and approved at least 7 days in advance. Leave in Ramadan, Eid and White Friday periods in stores is limited by the roster.

## 4. Other Leave

| Leave | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Sick leave | First 30 days at full pay, next 60 days at 75 percent, next 30 days unpaid, with a medical report |
| Maternity leave | 12 weeks at full pay |
| Paternity leave | 3 days |
| Marriage leave | 5 days |
| Bereavement leave | 5 days for a spouse, parent or child |
| Hajj leave | Once during employment after 2 years of service, up to 10 days |
| Public holidays | Founding Day, Eid al-Fitr, Arafat Day and Eid al-Adha, National Day |

## 5. Pay

- Monthly salaries are paid on the 25th of the month. When the 25th is not a working day, payment is made on the last working day before it. Paydays fall in the period 25 to 28.
- Compensation of individual employees is Restricted data (see KB-POL-16).
- Pay is reviewed once a year in January together with the performance review.

## 6. Performance Review

Each employee has objectives set at the start of the year, a mid-year check and an annual review. Ratings are Outstanding, Meets Expectations and Needs Improvement. Store and warehouse objectives include safety and accuracy measures.

## 7. End of Service

End-of-service award is paid according to the Saudi Labor Law: half a month of pay for each of the first five years and a full month for each year after that.

## 8. Recruitment

Vacancies are approved by the Chief Operating Officer and the Chief Financial Officer and filled by the HR Director's team. Saudi nationals are prioritised in line with the Saudization programme.
