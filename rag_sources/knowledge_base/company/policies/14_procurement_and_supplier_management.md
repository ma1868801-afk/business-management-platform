## 1. Principles

Nakhla Retail Group buys from 16 contracted suppliers, two for each product line. Having two suppliers per line limits dependence on a single source. Suppliers are selected for price, quality, reliability and compliance, and are managed through a written contract.

## 2. Supplier Contracts

- Supplier contracts have a term of 36 months and renew automatically for 12 months unless notice is given 90 days before the end.
- Each contract states the lead time, the minimum order quantity, the payment terms, the currency and the penalty for late delivery.
- Contract numbers follow the pattern SC-YYYY-NNN. Contracts are owned by the Procurement Manager.

## 3. Performance Targets

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| On-time delivery | 92 percent of purchase orders delivered on or before the expected delivery date |
| Quality score | At least 4.0 out of 5 |

Currently 2 suppliers are below 85 percent on-time delivery (SUP-04 (84 percent), SUP-08 (82.5 percent)) and 4 are below 90 percent. Suppliers below target are placed on an improvement plan reviewed every month by the Procurement Manager and the Supplier Quality Specialist.

## 4. Late Delivery Penalty

A delivery that arrives after the expected delivery date incurs a penalty of 0.5 percent of the value of the late line for each day of delay, capped at 5 percent of that line value. The penalty is deducted from the supplier's next payment.

## 5. Purchase Orders

- Purchase order numbers follow the pattern PO-YYYY-NNNN.
- The expected delivery date equals the order date plus the supplier's lead time.
- Quantities are rounded up to the supplier's minimum order quantity.
- Purchase order statuses are Open, In Transit, Partially Received, Received and Cancelled.
- The total of a purchase order is recorded in SAR. Suppliers may invoice in SAR, USD, EUR or AED; the amount is converted at the order date.

## 6. Approval Limits

| Purchase order value | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to SAR 50,000 | Procurement Manager |
| SAR 50,001 to SAR 250,000 | Head of Supply Chain |
| Above SAR 250,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

An order may not be split to stay below an approval limit.

## 7. Receiving and Payment

- Goods are received and checked at the Distribution Center against the purchase order.
- The Supplier Quality Specialist handles quality complaints and returns to suppliers.
- Invoices are paid within the payment terms of the contract (30, 45 or 60 days).

## 8. Review and Renewal

The Procurement Manager reviews each supplier every quarter. Renewal, termination or change of supplier is decided by the Head of Supply Chain with the Chief Operating Officer.
