# Nakhla Rewards Loyalty Programme

- Owner: Rakan Al-Anazi, Head of Marketing (EMP-0007)

- Version: 2.2
- Last reviewed: 2026-09-30
- Applies to: Individual customers and customer-facing staff
- Classification: Public

## 1. Programme

Nakhla Rewards is the company's loyalty programme for individual customers. Customers register in a store, on the website or in the Mobile App. Contract business customers do not join the programme; their tier is None and they receive their contract discount instead.

## 2. Tiers

A customer's tier is set by the net spend of the last 12 months, excluding VAT. The tier is recalculated every night.

| Tier | Trailing 12-month net spend (SAR) |
|---|---|
| None | Below 3,000 |
| Silver | 3,000 or more |
| Gold | 10,000 or more |
| Platinum | 25,000 or more |

## 3. Points

- A member earns 1 point for every SAR 10 of net spend (excluding VAT).
- 100 points can be redeemed for SAR 5 off a future purchase.
- Points remain valid while the account is active.
- Points are earned on In-Store, Online and Mobile App purchases, and are reversed when the purchase is returned.

## 4. Benefits

| Benefit | None | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Earn and redeem points | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Member discount on lines without a promotion | No | No | 5 percent | 10 percent |
| Free delivery on Online and Mobile App orders | Only from SAR 250 | Only from SAR 250 | Always | Always |

The delivery fee is SAR 25 for Online and Mobile App orders below SAR 250 placed by customers who are not Gold or Platinum members.

## 5. Rules

- The member discount is never combined with a promotional discount.
- Tier changes take effect the day after the nightly recalculation.
- The programme data (tier and points) is personal data and follows the data privacy policy (KB-POL-16).
- Questions about a customer's own tier are handled by Customer Service after identity verification.

## 6. Programme Size

About 45 percent of individual customers are in tier None, 30 percent in Silver, 18 percent in Gold and 7 percent in Platinum. Average order value rises with the tier.
