## 2. Role in the Network

Largest store and highest-revenue location; full range of all eight product lines; flagship for White Friday and Ramadan campaigns.

## 3. Management

The location is managed by Faisal Al-Harbi, Branch Manager, Riyadh Olaya Flagship, who reports to the Chief Operating Officer, Tariq Al-Shehri. Work email: faisal.alharbi@nakhla-retail.example. The manager is responsible for sales against target, stock accuracy, staffing, safety and customer service at the location.

## 4. Range and Channels

- Sells through the In-Store channel; Online, Mobile App and B2B orders are fulfilled by the Distribution Center, not by this store.
- Holds between 80 and 100 of the 120 SKUs.
- Opening hours: 10:00 to 23:00 daily.

## 5. Performance and Seasonality

- Ranked 1 of the 8 stores by 2025 net revenue.
- Leads the Ramadan and White Friday campaigns; Consumer Electronics and Home Decor are strongest in the White Friday window.
- The monthly sales target is the net revenue of the same month of the previous year multiplied by 1.10 (see KB-POL-22).
- Weekends (Friday and Saturday) and paydays (25th to 28th) are stronger days in all stores.

## 6. Replenishment

Is replenished from the Distribution Center according to the inventory policy (KB-POL-13). The Branch Manager reports damage, losses and count differences to the Inventory Planning team.
