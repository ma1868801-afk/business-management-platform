## 2. Role in the Network

Smallest store; summer demand is strong because Abha is a summer tourism destination in the Southern region.

## 3. Management

The location is managed by Mohammed Al-Asmari, Branch Manager, Abha City Express, who reports to the Chief Operating Officer, Tariq Al-Shehri. Work email: mohammed.alasmari@nakhla-retail.example. The manager is responsible for sales against target, stock accuracy, staffing, safety and customer service at the location.

## 4. Range and Channels

- Sells through the In-Store channel; Online, Mobile App and B2B orders are fulfilled by the Distribution Center, not by this store.
- Holds between 80 and 100 of the 120 SKUs.
- Opening hours: 11:00 to 23:00 daily; one hour later than the Flagship and Standard stores.

## 5. Performance and Seasonality

- Ranked 8 of the 8 stores by 2025 net revenue.
- Summer demand is strong because Abha is a summer tourism destination: June to August is at least 1.2 times its own monthly average. It is the lowest-revenue store in every year.
- The monthly sales target is the net revenue of the same month of the previous year multiplied by 1.10 (see KB-POL-22).
- Weekends (Friday and Saturday) and paydays (25th to 28th) are stronger days in all stores.

## 6. Replenishment

Is replenished from the Distribution Center according to the inventory policy (KB-POL-13). The Branch Manager reports damage, losses and count differences to the Inventory Planning team.
