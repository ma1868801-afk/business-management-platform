## 2. Role in the Network

Central warehouse for all stores; also fulfils Online, Mobile App and B2B orders, so online and B2B sales are attributed to this location.

## 3. Management

The location is managed by Khalid Al-Mutairi, Distribution Center Manager, who reports to the Chief Operating Officer, Tariq Al-Shehri. Work email: khalid.almutairi@nakhla-retail.example. The manager is responsible for sales against target, stock accuracy, staffing, safety and customer service at the location.

## 4. Range and Channels

- Holds all 120 SKUs of the eight product lines and supplies the eight stores.
- Fulfils all Online, Mobile App and B2B orders; those sales are attributed to `RYD-DC`.
- Receives most supplier deliveries and takes a stock snapshot every Saturday.
- Operates around the clock; the team and processes are described in the warehouse operations policy (KB-POL-15).

## 5. Performance and Seasonality

- Not ranked: it carries Online, Mobile App and B2B sales, so store rankings exclude it.
- Carries the White Friday online peak and the growing digital share of net revenue.
- The monthly sales target is the net revenue of the same month of the previous year multiplied by 1.10 (see KB-POL-22).
- Weekends (Friday and Saturday) and paydays (25th to 28th) are stronger days in all stores.

## 6. Replenishment

Receives supplier deliveries and replenishes the stores according to the inventory policy (KB-POL-13).
