## 2. Parties

The seller is Nakhla Retail Group, Riyadh. The customer is Gulf Catering Services, a catering organisation in Dammam. The customer buys through the B2B channel. Orders are fulfilled from the Riyadh Distribution Center and recorded under location `RYD-DC`.

## 3. Term and Renewal

- The initial term is 24 months, from 2023-08-01 to 2025-07-31.
- The agreement renews automatically for periods of 12 months unless one party gives written notice at least 60 days before the end of the current term.
- It has been renewed 2 times, from 2025-08-01, 2026-08-01.
- The current term ends on 2027-07-31. The notice date for the next renewal is 2027-06-01; without notice the agreement renews from 2027-08-01 to 2028-07-31.

## 4. Pricing and Discount

- The customer receives a contract discount of 12 percent on every order line, applied to the current list price excluding VAT.
- The contract discount replaces promotional and member discounts and never exceeds the discount cap of the product line.
- VAT of 15 percent is added to the discounted subtotal on the tax invoice.

## 5. Credit and Payment

- Credit limit: SAR 500,000. Open invoices must stay within this limit; an order that would exceed it is held.
- Payment terms: 30 days from the invoice date, by Bank Transfer. The customer receives a monthly consolidated statement and individual tax invoices.
- Invoices unpaid after the due date are reminded after 5 days and new orders are held after 30 days overdue.

## 6. Product Focus

The customer's main product lines are Grocery and Gourmet, Home Appliances. Other lines can be ordered at the same discount.

## 7. Ordering, Delivery and Returns

- Orders are placed with the account manager. Delivery to the customer's site is free of charge.
- Returns follow the returns policy (KB-POL-09): 14 days, or 30 days for defective items.
- The customer is not a member of the Nakhla Rewards programme; its loyalty tier is None.

## 8. Account Management

The account is managed by Turki Al-Harthi, B2B Account Manager, Eastern Region (EMP-0037), who reports to the Chief Commercial Officer. A review meeting is held every quarter to cover spend, payments and renewal.
