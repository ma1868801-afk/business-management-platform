## 2. Parties

The buyer is Nakhla Retail Group, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The supplier is Cairo Educational Supplies of Cairo, Egypt, supplier code SUP-13. The supplier delivers kids and school products to the Riyadh Distribution Center (`RYD-DC`).

## 3. Term and Renewal

- The initial term is 36 months from 2024-05-01 to 2027-04-30.
- The contract renews automatically for periods of 12 months unless one party gives written notice at least 90 days before the end of the current term.
- It has not been renewed yet; the initial term is the current term.
- The current term ends on 2027-04-30. The notice date for the next renewal is 2027-01-30; without notice the contract renews from 2027-05-01 to 2028-04-30.

## 4. Products Covered

The supplier provides 7 SKUs of the Kids and School line:

- KIDS-0001: SchoolPro Backpack 18-inch
- KIDS-0002: SchoolPro Rolling Backpack
- KIDS-0003: SchoolPro Lunch Box Set
- KIDS-0004: WriteWell Notebook Set 10-Pack
- KIDS-0005: WriteWell Pencil Case and Pens Kit
- KIDS-0006: ArtBox Colouring Set 72 pcs
- KIDS-0014: BrightKids Classic Geometry Set

## 5. Delivery Terms

- Lead time: 18 days. The expected delivery date of a purchase order equals the order date plus 18 days.
- Minimum order quantity: 200 units per order line; quantities are rounded up to this minimum.
- Delivery is to the Riyadh Distribution Center. A partial delivery is recorded as a partial receipt.

## 6. Price and Payment Terms

- Prices are agreed in USD. Purchase order values are converted to SAR at the order date for reporting.
- Payment is due 45 days after the invoice date.
- Price changes need 60 days written notice and the agreement of the Procurement Manager.

## 7. Performance and Penalties

- The target for on-time delivery is 92 percent. The supplier's measured on-time rate is 90.5 percent and is below the target.
- A late delivery incurs a penalty of 0.5 percent of the late line value for each day of delay, capped at 5 percent of that line value. The penalty is deducted from the next payment.
- The quality score is 4 out of 5. Defective or damaged goods are replaced or credited within 30 days.
- A supplier below 85 percent on-time delivery for two quarters in a row is placed on a formal improvement plan.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice before the end of a term. Nakhla Retail Group may terminate immediately for serious or repeated breach, including failure to correct an improvement plan.
