## 2. Parties

The buyer is Nakhla Retail Group, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The supplier is Levant Fashion House of Amman, Jordan, supplier code SUP-05. The supplier delivers fashion and apparel products to the Riyadh Distribution Center (`RYD-DC`).

## 3. Term and Renewal

- The initial term is 36 months from 2023-01-15 to 2026-01-14.
- The contract renews automatically for periods of 12 months unless one party gives written notice at least 90 days before the end of the current term.
- It has been renewed 1 time, from 2026-01-15.
- The current term ends on 2027-01-14. The notice date for the next renewal is 2026-10-16; without notice the contract renews from 2027-01-15 to 2028-01-14.

## 4. Products Covered

The supplier provides 9 SKUs of the Fashion and Apparel line:

- FASH-0001: Al Noor Classic White Thobe
- FASH-0002: Al Noor Premium Winter Thobe
- FASH-0003: Layali Embroidered Abaya
- FASH-0004: Layali Everyday Black Abaya
- FASH-0010: Layali Silk Scarf
- FASH-0011: Marasim Leather Handbag
- FASH-0012: Marasim Weekend Travel Bag
- FASH-0014: Marasim Evening Clutch (limited 2024 edition)
- FASH-0015: Layali Linen Kaftan

## 5. Delivery Terms

- Lead time: 14 days. The expected delivery date of a purchase order equals the order date plus 14 days.
- Minimum order quantity: 120 units per order line; quantities are rounded up to this minimum.
- Delivery is to the Riyadh Distribution Center. A partial delivery is recorded as a partial receipt.

## 6. Price and Payment Terms

- Prices are agreed in USD. Purchase order values are converted to SAR at the order date for reporting.
- Payment is due 45 days after the invoice date.
- Price changes need 60 days written notice and the agreement of the Procurement Manager.

## 7. Performance and Penalties

- The target for on-time delivery is 92 percent. The supplier's measured on-time rate is 93 percent and meets the target.
- A late delivery incurs a penalty of 0.5 percent of the late line value for each day of delay, capped at 5 percent of that line value. The penalty is deducted from the next payment.
- The quality score is 4.3 out of 5. Defective or damaged goods are replaced or credited within 30 days.
- A supplier below 85 percent on-time delivery for two quarters in a row is placed on a formal improvement plan.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice before the end of a term. Nakhla Retail Group may terminate immediately for serious or repeated breach, including failure to correct an improvement plan.

Contract owner: Wafa Al-Dosari, Procurement Manager (EMP-0012). Supplier quality contact: Majed Al-Subaie, Supplier Quality Specialist. Approval of purchase orders follows the approval limits in the procurement policy (KB-POL-14).
