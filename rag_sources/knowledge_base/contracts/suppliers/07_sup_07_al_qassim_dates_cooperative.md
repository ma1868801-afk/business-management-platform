## 2. Parties

The buyer is Nakhla Retail Group, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The supplier is Al Qassim Dates Cooperative of Buraydah, Saudi Arabia, supplier code SUP-07. The supplier delivers grocery and gourmet products to the Riyadh Distribution Center (`RYD-DC`).

## 3. Term and Renewal

- The initial term is 36 months from 2022-11-01 to 2025-10-31.
- The contract renews automatically for periods of 12 months unless one party gives written notice at least 90 days before the end of the current term.
- It has been renewed 2 times, from 2025-11-01, 2026-11-01.
- The current term ends on 2027-10-31. The notice date for the next renewal is 2027-08-02; without notice the contract renews from 2027-11-01 to 2028-10-31.

## 4. Products Covered

The supplier provides 7 SKUs of the Grocery and Gourmet line:

- GROC-0001: Qassim Gold Khalas Dates 1kg
- GROC-0002: Qassim Gold Sukkari Dates Gift Box 2kg
- GROC-0003: Nakhla Select Ramadan Essentials Basket
- GROC-0004: Qahwa House Arabic Coffee with Cardamom 500g
- GROC-0012: Qamar Al-Din Apricot Drink 1L
- GROC-0014: Nakhla Select Frozen Samosa Ramadan Pack 60 pcs
- GROC-0015: BeeBlessed Organic Honey 500g

## 5. Delivery Terms

- Lead time: 4 days. The expected delivery date of a purchase order equals the order date plus 4 days.
- Minimum order quantity: 300 units per order line; quantities are rounded up to this minimum.
- Delivery is to the Riyadh Distribution Center. A partial delivery is recorded as a partial receipt.

## 6. Price and Payment Terms

- Prices are agreed in SAR. Purchase orders are recorded in SAR.
- Payment is due 30 days after the invoice date.
- Price changes need 60 days written notice and the agreement of the Procurement Manager.

## 7. Performance and Penalties

- The target for on-time delivery is 92 percent. The supplier's measured on-time rate is 97.5 percent and meets the target.
- A late delivery incurs a penalty of 0.5 percent of the late line value for each day of delay, capped at 5 percent of that line value. The penalty is deducted from the next payment.
- The quality score is 4.7 out of 5. Defective or damaged goods are replaced or credited within 30 days.
- A supplier below 85 percent on-time delivery for two quarters in a row is placed on a formal improvement plan.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice before the end of a term. Nakhla Retail Group may terminate immediately for serious or repeated breach, including failure to correct an improvement plan.

Contract owner: Wafa Al-Dosari, Procurement Manager (EMP-0012). Supplier quality contact: Majed Al-Subaie, Supplier Quality Specialist. Approval of purchase orders follows the approval limits in the procurement policy (KB-POL-14).
