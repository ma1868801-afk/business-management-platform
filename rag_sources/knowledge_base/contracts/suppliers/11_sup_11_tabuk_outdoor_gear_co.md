## 2. Parties

The buyer is Nakhla Retail Group, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The supplier is Tabuk Outdoor Gear Co. of Tabuk, Saudi Arabia, supplier code SUP-11. The supplier delivers sports and outdoor products to the Riyadh Distribution Center (`RYD-DC`).

## 3. Term and Renewal

- The initial term is 36 months from 2024-02-01 to 2027-01-31.
- The contract renews automatically for periods of 12 months unless one party gives written notice at least 90 days before the end of the current term.
- It has not been renewed yet; the initial term is the current term.
- The current term ends on 2027-01-31. The notice date for the next renewal is 2026-11-02; without notice the contract renews from 2027-02-01 to 2028-01-31.

## 4. Products Covered

The supplier provides 8 SKUs of the Sports and Outdoor line:

- SPRT-0001: TrailNest 4-Person Camping Tent
- SPRT-0002: TrailNest 2-Person Camping Tent
- SPRT-0003: TrailNest Sleeping Bag (-5C)
- SPRT-0004: TrailNest Portable Camping Stove
- SPRT-0012: AquaGlide Swim Goggles
- SPRT-0013: PlayZone Football Size 5
- SPRT-0014: PlayZone Classic Tennis Racket
- SPRT-0015: Hydro Insulated Bottle 1L

## 5. Delivery Terms

- Lead time: 7 days. The expected delivery date of a purchase order equals the order date plus 7 days.
- Minimum order quantity: 50 units per order line; quantities are rounded up to this minimum.
- Delivery is to the Riyadh Distribution Center. A partial delivery is recorded as a partial receipt.

## 6. Price and Payment Terms

- Prices are agreed in SAR. Purchase orders are recorded in SAR.
- Payment is due 45 days after the invoice date.
- Price changes need 60 days written notice and the agreement of the Procurement Manager.

## 7. Performance and Penalties

- The target for on-time delivery is 92 percent. The supplier's measured on-time rate is 92.5 percent and meets the target.
- A late delivery incurs a penalty of 0.5 percent of the late line value for each day of delay, capped at 5 percent of that line value. The penalty is deducted from the next payment.
- The quality score is 4.2 out of 5. Defective or damaged goods are replaced or credited within 30 days.
- A supplier below 85 percent on-time delivery for two quarters in a row is placed on a formal improvement plan.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice before the end of a term. Nakhla Retail Group may terminate immediately for serious or repeated breach, including failure to correct an improvement plan.

Contract owner: Wafa Al-Dosari, Procurement Manager (EMP-0012). Supplier quality contact: Majed Al-Subaie, Supplier Quality Specialist. Approval of purchase orders follows the approval limits in the procurement policy (KB-POL-14).
