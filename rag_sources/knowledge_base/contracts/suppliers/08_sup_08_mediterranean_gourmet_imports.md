## 2. Parties

The buyer is Nakhla Retail Group, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The supplier is Mediterranean Gourmet Imports of Dubai, United Arab Emirates, supplier code SUP-08. The supplier delivers grocery and gourmet products to the Riyadh Distribution Center (`RYD-DC`).

## 3. Term and Renewal

- The initial term is 36 months from 2024-03-01 to 2027-02-28.
- The contract renews automatically for periods of 12 months unless one party gives written notice at least 90 days before the end of the current term.
- It has not been renewed yet; the initial term is the current term.
- The current term ends on 2027-02-28. The notice date for the next renewal is 2026-11-30; without notice the contract renews from 2027-03-01 to 2028-02-29.

## 4. Products Covered

The supplier provides 8 SKUs of the Grocery and Gourmet line:

- GROC-0005: GoldenGrain Premium Basmati Rice 5kg
- GROC-0006: Olivia Extra Virgin Olive Oil 1L
- GROC-0007: PureFarm Long Life Milk 1L (12 pack)
- GROC-0008: AquaPure Sparkling Water 330ml (24 pack)
- GROC-0009: NutHouse Mixed Nuts Tin 800g
- GROC-0010: Chocolat Royal Belgian Chocolate Selection Box
- GROC-0011: Zafran Co Saffron Threads 5g
- GROC-0013: Bellini Pasta 500g (12 pack)

## 5. Delivery Terms

- Lead time: 9 days. The expected delivery date of a purchase order equals the order date plus 9 days.
- Minimum order quantity: 250 units per order line; quantities are rounded up to this minimum.
- Delivery is to the Riyadh Distribution Center. A partial delivery is recorded as a partial receipt.

## 6. Price and Payment Terms

- Prices are agreed in AED. Purchase order values are converted to SAR at the order date for reporting.
- Payment is due 30 days after the invoice date.
- Price changes need 60 days written notice and the agreement of the Procurement Manager.

## 7. Performance and Penalties

- The target for on-time delivery is 92 percent. The supplier's measured on-time rate is 82.5 percent and is below the target.
- A late delivery incurs a penalty of 0.5 percent of the late line value for each day of delay, capped at 5 percent of that line value. The penalty is deducted from the next payment.
- The quality score is 3.9 out of 5. Defective or damaged goods are replaced or credited within 30 days.
- A supplier below 85 percent on-time delivery for two quarters in a row is placed on a formal improvement plan.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice before the end of a term. Nakhla Retail Group may terminate immediately for serious or repeated breach, including failure to correct an improvement plan.
